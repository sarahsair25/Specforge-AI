
# SpecForge AI

> **Bridging the Hardware Translation Gap with AI-Native Engineering.**

SpecForge AI is an agentic engineering platform designed to automate the transition from unstructured conceptual sketches to ISO-compliant, factory-ready manufacturing documentation. 

By leveraging a proprietary **Vision-to-Tolerance (V2T)** pipeline, SpecForge AI extracts geometric relationships from images and generates precise engineering specifications (GD&T, BOMs, Material Specs) that are validated for manufacturability via **Accio Work** before being routed to the **Alibaba.com** supplier ecosystem.

## 🛠️ The V2T Pipeline (Vision-to-Tolerance)
SpecForge AI is built on a multi-agent orchestration layer designed for "Production-Ready" AI:

1. **Vision Agent:** Extracts geometric and dimensional intent from raw conceptual inputs.
2. **Engineering Agent:** Applies ISO-1101 standards to generate structured manufacturing documentation.
3. **Validation Agent:** Integrates with the **Accio Work** ecosystem to verify manufacturability against real-world supplier constraints.
4. **Orchestrator Agent:** Manages quality control and cross-agent consistency to ensure measurable, factory-ready outputs.
